## The Meeting Nobody Has Time For

Every restaurant with servers should run a pre-shift huddle. Who is on. What we are out of. What the special is.

We do not always get the time. Servers come in at different times. So the meeting turns into a whiteboard in the back, or somebody's memory. The server who comes in at four never hears what the one at two was told.

## What I Asked For

I opened my Square Dashboard and told Squaremates what a manager needs at four o'clock. Three things.

1. **Who is on.** Every team member scheduled for the day, with their job and start time.
2. **Every 86'd item.** Anything out of stock or marked sold out.
3. **The special of the day.** Any item in the Specials category, with its price, that is live on the website.

There was nothing on the screen when I started. No template. I did not name an API. I did not explain the catalog. I asked the way a chef would ask.

## What It Built

It built the report. Then I asked it to turn the report into an app, so I can go to it once a day at the start of the shift.

It built the app and tested it against the day's real data. Then it asked me to approve it before it published.

Now the report lives at a link. My team opens it with the Square login they already have, on their own permissions. I do not hand over my whole dashboard. I can refresh it during the shift. When I want it to show something different, I tell it what I need.

The video is the whole build, on my own cafe's account. The waits are fast-forwarded and team member names are blurred. The rest is what you would see in your own dashboard.

## The Take

A whiteboard gets erased. A link does not.

The information was always there. The schedule was in Square. The sold-out items were in Square. The special was in Square. What was missing was somebody with twenty minutes to pull it together before every shift.

Nobody has those twenty minutes. Now nobody needs them.
